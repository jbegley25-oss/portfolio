Figure 4

Christie’s 1867 patent for plate molds.

Although Christie had the earliest patent, plate molds were used by the 1840s on soda,

mineral water, ale, and some other bottle types – including Citrate of Magnesia bottles.

Although these plates were usually rectangular, the Christie patent apparently instigated the use

of embossing read with the bottle on its side and opened the door to popularity for oval and

rectangular drug store ware (Lindsey 2014).
